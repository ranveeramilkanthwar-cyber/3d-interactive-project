## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a high-precision biomechanical surgical and diagnostic atmosphere. Tailored for orthopedic surgeons, neurosurgeons, radiologic technicians, and biomechanical researchers, the interface serves as a dark diagnostic suite for interactive 3D spine and spinal cord visualization. 

The aesthetic is futuristic medical instrumentation: surgical-grade dark mode, luminous telemetry, stereotactic volumetric planes, and micro-calibrated readout surfaces. Rather than consumer health softness or harsh sci-fi neon, the emotional tone delivers clinical authority, surgical focus, and ultra-high anatomical fidelity. 

Key attributes:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** True deep obsidian space allowing 3D bone geometry, neural pathways, and volumetric density maps to stand forward without visual fatigue.
- **Micro-Precision HUD:** Fine 1px hairline delimiters, stereotactic coordinate markings, and crisp numeric readouts reminiscent of precision surgical robotics.
- **Spectral Functional Luminescence:** Luminescent accents are strictly tied to anatomical significance—cervical, thoracic, lumbar, and sacrococcygeal segments, along with pathology heatmaps and nerve root tension metrics.

## Colors

The palette simulates a deep surgical operating suite with high-resolution holographic monitoring displays. The baseline is founded upon pure obsidian and dense slate, allowing luminescent cyan and electric medical blue to project active states and 3D coordinate vectors.

### Surface Architecture
- **Canvas Base (Obsidian Void):** `#06090E` – Deepest canvas floor for the 3D viewport canvas.
- **Surface Level 1 (Deep Slate):** `#0B0F17` – Primary background for structural chrome, HUD docks, and navigation panels.
- **Surface Level 2 (Sub-panel Slate):** `#111827` – Nested tool cards, segment inspector trays, and telemetry pods.
- **Surface Level 3 (Active Hover / Elevated):** `#1E293B` – State hovers, modal overlays, and elevated diagnostic popovers.
- **Border / Hairline Delimiter:** `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.15)` – Ghost hairlines defining UI boundaries.

### Diagnostic & Luminescent Tones
- **Primary (Electric Cyan):** `#00F0FF` – Active vertebral selection, targeted nerve vectors, focal surgical reticles, and primary interactive states.
- **Secondary (Medical Cerulean):** `#0EA5E9` – Secondary active instruments, rotational gyros, and persistent telemetry highlights.
- **Tertiary (Cold Steel Sky):** `#38BDF8` – Subordinate indicators, inactive coordinate axes, and informational callouts.
- **Bone Ivory (Luminescent Structural):** `#F1F5F9` – Primary anatomical typography, axial labels, and bone structure density values.
- **Muted Steel (Calibrated Inactive):** `#64748B` – Secondary telemetry metadata, inactive vertebrae, and grid rulers.

### Segment Color System (Spinal Topology)
- **Cervical (C1–C7):** `#38BDF8` (Luminous Cyan-Sky)
- **Thoracic (T1–T12):** `#00F0FF` (High-Frequency Medical Cyan)
- **Lumbar (L1–L5):** `#0284C7` (Dense Cobalt Blue)
- **Sacrum & Coccyx (S1–Co1):** `#6366F1` (Deep Neural Indigo)
- **Critical / Pathology Alert:** `#FF3366` (Hyper-luminescent Crimson for stenosis, disc herniation, and nerve impingement)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on three distinct functional font pairings:
1. **Space Grotesk (Primary Display & Structural Titles):** Provides a technical, geometric voice suited for high-tech instrument branding, anatomical section headers, and major modality switchers.
2. **Geist (Body Copy & Diagnostic Narrative):** A hyper-clean, neutral sans-serif designed for developer and precision interfaces. It offers optimal optical clarity for surgical case studies, patient pathology logs, and clinical metadata.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Telemetry, Coordinates, & Vertebral Tags):** Used for all numerical readings, degree measurements (e.g., Cobb angle, lordosis/kyphosis degrees), vertebral indices (`C1`, `T4`, `L5-S1`), cross-sectional coordinates, and slice indicators.

### Treatment Rules
- All numeric readouts, angles, coordinates, and spine level indicators must use `label-*` roles with tabular numbers enabled.
- Uppercase styling with expanded letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) is reserved for anatomical segment tags (`CERVICAL`, `THORACIC`, `LUMBAR`, `SACRAL`) and telemetry units.

## Layout & Spacing

The UI functions as an instrumentation cockpit with the 3D viewport canvas acting as the focal viewport, overlaid by modular diagnostic docking panels.

### Viewport & HUD Architecture
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** A 16-column HUD matrix. Outer left column (width: 320px) houses the Spinal Column Navigator (hierarchical vertebra picker from C1 to Coccyx). Center viewport is an expansive 3D interactive stage. Outer right column (width: 380px) houses the Biomechanical Telemetry & Cross-Section Inspector. Top-floating HUD strip displays patient axial planes and rotational transforms.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout. The right telemetry drawer collapses into a bottom-docked slide sheet with tabbed metrics. The left vertebra selector condenses to a vertical iconic spine ruler.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column presentation. The 3D spine occupies 65vh with floating translucent overlay chips for segmental jumping. Telemetry and pathology readouts are accessible via a gesture-driven pull-up diagnostic sheet.

### Spacing Philosophy
Consistent 4px/8px incremental rhythm (`0.25rem` to `2rem`). HUD panels use tight internal padding (`space-sm` to `space-md`) to optimize data density and eliminate wasted screen real estate, maintaining an analytical environment.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through translucent dark tonal layering, laser-thin structural outlines, and optical photonic glows rather than traditional diffuse drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Layering
1. **Viewport Floor (Ground Zero):** `#06090E` solid base containing the WebGL/Three.js 3D spine render with ambient depth fog.
2. **Docking HUD Surfaces:** `rgba(11, 15, 23, 0.82)` background with `16px` backdrop-filter blur. Outlined by a crisp `1px` border of `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.12)`.
3. **Floating Overlay Cards & Tooltips:** `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.94)` background with `20px` backdrop-filter blur. Outlined by `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25)`.

### Photonic Glow & Focus Signals
- **Vertebral Focus Halo:** When an individual vertebra (e.g., L4) is selected in 3D space, associated cards emit a focused cyan photonic rim light: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.28), inset 0 0 12px 0 rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Pathology Flare:** Degenerative disc, bone spur, or stenosis alerts use an emergency crimson rim: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -2px rgba(255, 51, 102, 0.35)`.
- **No Heavy Drop Shadows:** Avoid muddy dark drop shadows that obscure visual clarity in low-key dark interfaces; depth is strictly articulated via border luminosity and surface transradiancy.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a calibrated, technical shape profile using low-radius geometric curvature (`roundedness: 1`, `0.25rem` / `4px` baseline). 

- Small actionable HUD tags, vertebra pills, and telemetry chips carry `4px` corner radii.
- Diagnostic master panels, modal inspector frames, and viewport HUD overlays use `8px` (`rounded-lg`).
- Interactive reticles, rotational gimbal rings, and cross-section depth crosshairs use pure circular geometries (`50%` radius).
- Avoid soft or bubbly curves (`roundedness: 2` or `3`). The interface must convey surgical millimeter accuracy.

## Components

### Buttons & Surgical Controls
- **Primary Kinetic Action:** Background `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.12)`, 1px border `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.6)`, text `#00F0FF`, font `label-md`. Hover state increases surface fill to `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.24)` with a `0 0 16px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.4)` outer aura.
- **Secondary Instrumentation:** Background `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.7)`, border `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.18)`, text `#F1F5F9`. Hover state shifts border to `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4)`.
- **Iconic Telemetry Tools:** 36x36px square controls with 4px border radius for 3D viewport resets, coronal/sagittal/axial plane snaps, and mesh wireframe toggles.

### Vertebral Segment Selector (Spine Ribbon)
- A segmented vertical track mapping C1–C7, T1–T12, L1–L5, and S1–Coccyx.
- Each vertebra entry is displayed with its identifier (`C5`, `L3`) in `label-sm` JetBrains Mono.
- State: Inactive displays muted steel (`#64748B`). Hover triggers `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.15)` background fill. Active selection highlights with a high-intensity cyan tag, bone ivory text (`#F1F5F9`), and a left-aligned 2px neon cyan indicator notch.

### Biomechanical Data Cards
- Container built from `Surface Level 2` with `1px` hairline boundary.
- **Header:** Uppercase `label-sm` with sub-segment color code (e.g., sky cyan for Cervical, cobalt for Lumbar), right-aligned with coordinate telemetry (`X: 0.04 Y: 1.12 Z: -0.84`).
- **Telemetry Readout:** High-contrast `headline-lg` numeric values (`42.8°` Cobb Angle, `3.2mm` Canal Diameter) rendered in Bone Ivory with cyan-tinted units.

### Anatomic Status Chips & Badges
- Pill-form badges with 4px radius, `8px` horizontal and `2px` vertical padding.
- Consists of a 6px pulsing LED indicator dot on the left followed by capitalized `label-sm` text.
- Healthy disc: `#0EA5E9` (Stable Cerulean).
- Degenerative / Spondylolisthesis: `#FF3366` (Crimson Warning).
- Artificially Instrumented (Cages, Pedicle Screws): `#F59E0B` (Telemetry Amber).

### Inputs & Cross-Section Sliders
- Range sliders for CT/MRI bone opacity and tissue peeling use a 2px track with `#1E293B` background and `#00F0FF` fill. Thumb is an 12x12px square diamond rotated 45° with a cyan glow.
- Numeric coordinate inputs feature monospaced JetBrains Mono text with micro adjustment buttons (+/-) built directly into the hairline border frame.

### 3D Spatial Reticles & Pinpoint Annotations
- Floating canvas labels anchored to 3D bone nodes.
- Connected via a 1px angled leader line (`rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.5)`).
- Anchor point is an animated pulsing concentric ring; the annotation box is a micro-card (`rgba(6, 9, 14, 0.85)`) displaying vertebrae tag and nerve branch name.
